Frequently asked questions

Everything worth knowing about renting therapy rooms at Płaszowska 25 in Kraków: prices, rental options, furnishings and booking.

Who are the rooms at Płaszowska 25 for?

For psychologists, psychotherapists, couples and family therapists, speech and neuro-speech therapists, sexologists, coaches and addiction therapists, as well as supervisors and group facilitators. We work both with experienced professionals and with people who are starting their own practice.

What rental options are there?

Two. You can rent a recurring slot on a specific day of the week (a half-day or full-day block, the same room and time every week) or a whole room for your exclusive use on a permanent lease, available 7 days a week.

How much does it cost to rent a therapy room in Kraków?

We rent in blocks (gross prices): morning 6:00–15:00 from 250 PLN (small room) / 350 PLN (large); afternoon 15:00–23:00 from 299 PLN / 399 PLN; full day 6:00–23:00 from 500 PLN / 600 PLN. Monthly rental is priced individually, at preferential rates.

What is included in the price?

A fully furnished room (armchairs, and a couch in selected rooms), good lighting and acoustics, a shared waiting room for clients, plus cleaning and facilities. No hidden fees.

Where is the centre and how do I get there?

Płaszowska 25, 30-713 Kraków (Płaszów district), an intimate centre in a well-connected part of the city, about 10 minutes from the city centre.

How do I book a room?

Check availability in the live schedule and send an enquiry through the booking form, or call +48 510 574 421. We usually call back within one working day and arrange a viewing.
